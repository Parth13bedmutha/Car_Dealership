# Project Problem Statement & Scope

## 1. Problem Statement

Small to mid-sized independent automotive dealerships often struggle with managing inventory and tracking financial performance due to complex or overly expensive software systems. Manual record-keeping methods (such as spreadsheets or physical ledgers) are prone to human error, lack real-time visibility into total vehicle investment costs (purchase price combined with repair expenses), and make calculating overall net profitability tedious. There is a clear need for a simple, lightweight system that allows dealership managers to track vehicle status, calculate transaction margins, and evaluate overall dealership profitability seamlessly.

## 2. Scope of the Project

* **Vehicle Inventory Management:** Adding new inventory items with detailed metadata (VIN, Make, Model, Year, Purchase Price, and Repair Expenses).

* **Automated Cost Calculation:** Automatic aggregation of purchase costs and repair fees into total vehicle unit cost.

* **Sales & Transaction Processing:** Marking vehicles as sold, recording final sale prices, and calculating profit/loss on individual sales.

* **Financial Reporting:** Generating a summarized Profit & Loss (P&L) report showing overall revenue, expenses, and net profit across completed transactions.

## 3. Target Users

* **Independent Car Dealership Owners:** Business owners looking for quick insight into net profit and operational margins.

* **Inventory & Fleet Managers:** Staff members responsible for logging newly acquired vehicles, tracking repair expenses, and updating stock availability.

* **Sales Representatives:** Staff members executing sales transactions and recording sale prices in real time.

## 4. High-Level Features

* **Real-time Inventory Directory:** Instantly displays available stock along with listing prices and VIN identification.

* **Dynamic Cost & Margin Tracker:** Computes total cost per car and immediate transaction return .

* **Automated Profit & Loss (P&L) Reporting:** Summarizes total dealership revenue, cumulative expenses, and net profit/loss across all recorded transactions.

* **Interactive CLI Menu:** User-friendly menu structure with input error handling to ensure seamless menu navigation.
